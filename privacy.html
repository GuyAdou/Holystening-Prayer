Privacy Policy

Holystening is a distraction-free prayer app built to work entirely on your device. This page explains, plainly, what that means for your data.

Effective August 19, 2026 Applies to the Holystening iOS app Publisher Foresee
The short version

Holystening does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your data — because it never leaves your device in the first place.

  • No account, no sign-up, no personal information requested.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind.
  • No network connection of any kind — the app never talks to a server, ours or anyone else's.
  • Any preferences you set are stored only in local storage on your iPhone.
01

What Holystening does

Holystening plays a bundled instrumental track during timed prayer sessions and offers a local Bible reader. Both run using content packaged inside the app itself — nothing is fetched from the internet, because the app has no networking code at all.

02

Information we do not collect

We do not collect, and have no way to collect, any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, or any other identifying information
  • Location, contacts, photos, health data, or calendar information
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or behavioral tracking
  • Advertising identifiers, or any data used for ad targeting
  • How you use the app

There is no analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or third-party library of any kind built into Holystening. There is nothing for us to receive, because nothing is ever sent.

03

What's stored on your device

A small amount of data lives only in your iPhone's local storage, managed entirely by iOS and never transmitted anywhere:

DataPurposeWhere it lives
Onboarding & playback preferencesRemembering setup steps and your loop/track choicesOn-device only, local settings
Focus-mode connection flagRemembering that you linked an iOS FocusOn-device only, local settings

None of this is backed up to any server we control, synced to any account, or accessible to us in any form. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

04

iOS Focus integration

Holystening can optionally connect to an iOS Focus you choose, using Apple's Focus Filter system. This connection is set up entirely by you, inside Apple's own Settings app, and is used only to silence notifications while you pray. It does not give Holystening — or us — access to your Focus settings, your other apps, or any additional data.

05

Audio & microphone

The prayer music is a single instrumental track bundled inside the app. Holystening plays audio to your device's speakers or headphones — it does not request microphone access, and does not record, listen to, or process any sound.

06

Children's privacy

Holystening is not directed at children under 13, and because the app collects no data from anyone — of any age — there is nothing to knowingly collect from a child either.

07

Changes to this policy

If Holystening ever changes in a way that affects this policy — for example, if a future version adds a feature that uses the network — this page will be updated first, with a new effective date above, before that change ships.
